Network segments

{{if .Data.FirstRun}}
Your account is set up. One more recommended step: keep smart devices and guests off your own network.
{{end}}

On one flat network, a hacked camera or a guest's infected laptop can reach every other device. A segment is a separate network with its own addresses and DHCP that can reach the internet and nothing else. Each one here is a VLAN on the LAN port ({{or .Data.Parent "?"}}): set your access point to put the IoT and guest Wi-Fi on these VLAN IDs (or use a VLAN-capable switch).
