VPN (WireGuard)

Reach your network -- and manage this router -- from anywhere, with a key per device instead of a password. A stolen password works from anywhere; a WireGuard key only on the device that holds it, and the router only ever knows its public half. This is the safe alternative to opening the webUI to the internet.
