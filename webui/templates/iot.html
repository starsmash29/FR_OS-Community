IoT Devices

Discovers devices in the selected zones (DHCP leases, the ARP table and an mDNS service query), classifies them by hardware vendor, advertised services and hostname, and can isolate IoT devices by MAC address in the router's own firewall. Isolation covers everything routed through this box; devices on the same switch/Wi-Fi segment can still reach each other directly -- put IoT gear in its own zone/VLAN for full separation.
